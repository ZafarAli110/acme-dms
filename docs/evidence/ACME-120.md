## What these checks don't prove

- A close name's customers are found by the corrected word anywhere in the search text, as the search finds them, so
  a close company word can bring a longer word with it ("oppe" → "Ouse" also brings "Brighouse Bakery"). Close, but
  broader than the name alone; a person should judge whether it matters.
- The export holding only the exact matches (the ticket's open point) was not checked live: it needs an export job.
- The name list's triggers were checked by the test, not by renaming live customers.
- The divider's light-blue band in a.html shows as blue text on white in the app.
