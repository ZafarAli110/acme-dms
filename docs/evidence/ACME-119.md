## What these checks don't prove

- Exactly 10,000 matches is checked only by the handler test ("counts exactly up to 10,000 and stops at 10,001"): no
  live data matches exactly 10,000. Nothing more needed: the test runs in the gate.
- French ("10 000+") is checked only by the formatter's unit test, not on screen, and the export dialog was not
  photographed at 1024. A person should look once in French.
- The busy-morning rate is a laptop's (about 130 a second holds): it compares builds, not production's 3,300 a second
  (ACME-117's later steps).
- The browser was used as sam and maria only; rita and tom were checked through the API.
