## Requirement

ACME-117, in the ticket's words: "nothing is lost when one stops". Stopping a dealership-api copy during
`load-test --rate 100` timed out 10 of 4,500 requests (ACME-121's evidence): the gateway kept sending to the copy that
had gone, and Aspire's local proxy holds a connection to a stopped process open until the gateway's 15 s limit.

- **In:** the gateway checks each copy's liveness every few seconds and takes a copy out at its first failed or slow
  answer (a copy shutting down answers 503 at once, `return503OnClosing`); passive checks take out a copy whose
  requests fail.
- **Out:** a retry of a request already sent to a copy that has gone (YARP has none built in; a later step if the
  measurement still loses requests); readiness as the probe (a slow database would take every copy out at once).

- [x] The database slow or down → copies are not taken out by the gateway (the probe is liveness, which never touches
